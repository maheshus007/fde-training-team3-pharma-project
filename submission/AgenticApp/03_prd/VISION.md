## Problem statement

Authorized Quality, PV and Supply reviewers waste cycle time reconciling contradictory, poorly provenanced evidence across brownfield systems while board pressure (BR-01) and hard gates forbid transferring regulated decisions to automation.

## Governing outcome

Deliver an offline-capable advisory agentic workbench that cites ontology-governed, KG-linked evidence for Workflows A/B/C, abstains under ambiguity, and never executes disposition, final PV judgements, or stock side effects.
